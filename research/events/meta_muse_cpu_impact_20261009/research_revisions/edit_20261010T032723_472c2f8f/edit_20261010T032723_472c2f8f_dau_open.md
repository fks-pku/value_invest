L103: cite5†AI L104: # Meta’s Muse is outpacing ChatGPT’s early mobile launch
L105: 
L106: cite46†Sarah Perez L107: 
L108: 12:19 PM PDT · September 21, 2026
L109: 
L110: Meta’s latest AI app, Muse, could be the social giant’s next major hit, new data suggests. According to just- released estimates from market intelligence provider cite47†Apptopia†apptopia.com , Muse’s app on mobile devices has been downloaded more times during its first 12 days on the market than ChatGPT was in the 12 days after its debut. (This data compares the U.S. and Canada markets only.)
L111: Muse also has more daily active users than ChatGPT had at the time, the firm found.
L112: 
L113: Until now, it had been difficult to make an exact apples-to-apples comparison between these two apps, given that they had pursued different launch strategies. When ChatGPT arrived on mobile, it was made available globally, but only on iOS. Muse, meanwhile, is available across both Apple’s App Store and Google Play, but only in the U.S. and Canada.
L114: To better align the numbers for comparison, Apptopia looked only at the iOS data for the U.S. and Canada for both apps during the first 12 days of their respective launches. In this subset of the data, Muse has now seen 1.8 million downloads to ChatGPT’s 1.3 million.
L115: Overall, Muse has seen 2.8 million total installs globally in its first 12 days, the firm also said. Its growth hasn’t yet stagnated, either; Muse has moved up from its original position as No. 2 overall on the U.S. App Store immediately after its launch cite48†to now No. 1†appfigures.com , as cite49†Business Insider reported†www.businessinsider.com on Friday. That jump put the app higher than ChatGPT, the outlet noted.
L116: Another firm, cite48†Appfigures†appfigures.com , said at the time that Muse had then crossed 1 million downloads.
L117: cite50†Image Image Credits:Appfigures
L118: 
L119: In addition, Apptopia’s data indicates that Muse’s U.S. daily active users are now higher than they were for ChatGPT at the same point after its launch. When comparing just the U.S. mobile app daily active users, Muse comes in higher with 642,000 daily users compared with 231,000 for ChatGPT at the time.
