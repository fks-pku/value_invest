L22: The difference is particularly relevant for a persistent AI agent. Such activity can still represent meaningful product engagement, but it measures a different behavior from initiating new work.
L23: Downloads provide a third measure of Muse’s initial momentum. cite18†Sensor Tower estimated†thenextweb.com that Muse exceeded five million downloads within approximately 22 days of launch.
