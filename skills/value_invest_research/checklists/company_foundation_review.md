# Company Foundation Review Checklist

Before treating a company as research-ready, confirm:

- Source and origin: the original business wedge and company DNA are clear.
- History: major business, governance, financing, M&A, and capital-allocation events are dated.
- Current business: revenue model, customers, demand, margins, and cash conversion are described with evidence.
- Value chain: supplier, channel, customer, and substitute power are mapped.
- Competition: peers, market position, share trend, and barriers are stated without vague moat language.
- Strategy: mission, corporate, competitive, and functional strategy are distinguished.
- Organization and governance: incentives, ownership, leadership, culture, and governance risks are explicit.
- Risk sweep: thesis breakers and missing evidence are listed before any positive conclusion.

If two or more sections are materially incomplete, output `foundation_status: incomplete` and avoid a strengthened stock view.
