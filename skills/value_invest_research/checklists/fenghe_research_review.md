# FengHe Research Review Checklist

Before strengthening a thesis, confirm:

- Cycle: the cycle state is stated and not treated as permanent growth.
- Change: the marginal change is observable, dated, and tied to evidence.
- Certainty: uncertainty is named directly; upside alone is not used as the reason.
- D driver: D1, D2, or D3 is identified as the dominant current price driver.
- 5M: at least one M explains value creation, and at least one M is tested as a defect risk.
- 3T: the time frame matches the evidence horizon.
- Risk: the thesis has explicit disconfirming tests and human review actions.
