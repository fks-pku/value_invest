# FengHe Event Research Framework

Use this for sudden shocks such as geopolitical conflict, regulatory action, commodity disruption, product breakthroughs, or financial stress. Events are not conclusions; they are possible D2 changes that must be tested through 3C, 3D, 5M, and 3T.

Process:

1. State confirmed facts.
2. Separate unconfirmed claims and rumors.
3. State 3C: cycle context, marginal change, and certainty.
4. Build transmission chains from event to economic mechanism.
5. Map affected sectors, themes, and companies.
6. Screen candidates by direct exposure, 5M sensitivity, valuation, and market pricing.
7. Identify each candidate's dominant 3D driver and 3T time frame.
8. Rank candidates into Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, and Negative Watch.
9. Promote only Tier 1 candidates with evidence-backed mechanisms into stock-level memo updates.

Do not jump directly from news headline to ticker conclusion.
