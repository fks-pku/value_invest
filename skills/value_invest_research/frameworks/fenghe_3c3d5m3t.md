# FengHe 3C3D5M3T Framework

This is the message-flow and thesis-change framework for this project. For individual companies, use it after the company foundation baseline; for sectors and events, use it to organize cycle, change, certainty, drivers, and time frames.

Source reference: https://xueqiu.com/5739488179/363268127

## 3C: Investment Philosophy

- Cycle: Everything is cyclical. Do not treat growth, quality, or management excellence as permanent.
- Change: Invest in observable change. Identify what is improving or deteriorating at the margin.
- Certainty: Certainty outranks upside. A lower-return but evidence-backed idea is preferred to a high-upside uncertain idea.

Every research conclusion must answer:

- What cycle is this company, sector, or event inside?
- What changed, and is the change structural or cyclical?
- What evidence creates certainty, and what remains uncertain?

## 3D: Price Drivers

- D1: ROE and intrinsic value. Cash-generating ability, book value growth, and durable returns.
- D2: External or marginal change. Inflection points, catalysts, share shifts, margin changes, and expectation revisions.
- D3: Sentiment and valuation. Market emotion, valuation multiples, crowding, and price dislocation.

Every message-flow stock view must identify the dominant current driver. Do not mix D1, D2, and D3 into vague language.

## 5M: Value Analysis

- M1: Market size. Total addressable market, cycle runway, and saturation risk.
- M2: Market share. Share gains/losses, competitive position, and distribution power.
- M3: Margin. Gross margin, operating leverage, cost discipline, and pricing power.
- M4: Model. Business model, cash conversion, reinvestment need, and capital intensity.
- M5: Management. Capital allocation, incentives, execution, culture, and communication quality.

Use 5M to explain ROE. A strong claim about value must name which M drives it and which M could break it.

## 3T: Time Frame

- T1: 0-3 months. Short-term catalyst, event reaction, or sentiment/valuation move.
- T2: 3-15 months. Earnings revision, cycle confirmation, margin/share inflection, or valuation normalization.
- T3: 15+ months. Long-cycle value creation, business model proof, and durable ROE compounding.

Every thesis change must state its time frame. A T1 idea cannot rely mainly on T3 evidence, and a T3 idea cannot depend on short-lived sentiment.

## Output Discipline

Prefer "needs_review" when evidence does not support a FengHe conclusion. A valid output separates:

- Cycle state.
- Change type and evidence.
- Certainty level and uncertainty.
- Dominant D driver.
- 5M drivers and defects.
- T time frame.
- Disconfirming tests.
- Human review actions.
