# FengHe Sector And Theme Research Framework

Use this to understand an industry, theme, value chain, or cross-sector trend through FengHe 3C3D5M3T.

Process:

1. Define the research object and scope.
2. State 3C: the cycle state, marginal change, and certainty level.
3. Map industry structure, value chain, profit pools, and barriers to entry.
4. Identify demand drivers, cycle variables, and key metrics.
5. Build a company map: leaders, challengers, niche compounders, cyclical candidates, and avoid/watch names.
6. Compare companies through 5M: market size, market share, margin, model, and management.
7. For each candidate, identify dominant 3D driver and 3T time frame.
8. Output candidate companies for stock-level research.
