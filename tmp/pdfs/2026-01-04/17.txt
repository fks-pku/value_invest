China CPO: RoboTechnik (300757.SZ; NC): Mgmt. call: SiPh driving 
growth; opto-electronic components automated assembly and testing 
30 December 2025 | 8:10PM HKT
We talked to RoboTechnik’s (300757.SZ, Not Covered) management recently. 
RoboTechnik entered the CPO (Co-Packaged Optics) market via the acquisition of 
FiconTEC (private, Announcement link), which is a global leader in automated 
assembly and testing systems for integrated photonic devices and high-end 
opto-electronic components. In Oct 2025, FiconTEC launched DLT-D1, a multi-site 
die-level tester for double-sided compact 3D CPO optical engines, capturing the 
demand for 2.5D and 3D CPO architectures in AI infrastructure (link). Management 
remains positive on its high-precision automated equipment for assembly and 
testing, and aims to expand capacity ahead.  
Read across to Optical modules: Management’s positive tone on the end demand 
for SiPh (Silicon Photonics) optical modules echoes our positive view on networking 
under AI infrastructure cycle. SiPh carries a more compact and simple structure that 
reduces components costs and allows better production efficiency, and we expect to 
see rising contribution from Silicon Photonics in 800G/ 1.6T optical modules. Buy: 
Innolight, Eoptolink, Landmark, VPEC, TFC Optical.  
Company profile: RoboTechnik (300757.SZ) was established in 2011 and started 
with the photovoltaic cell manufacturing equipment business. In 2020, the company 
invested in FiconTEC, a German company providing automated assembly and testing 
equipment for integrated photonic devices and high-end opto-electronic 
components, leading the company to enter SiPh / CPO market, participating in AI 
infrastructure. FiconTEC becomes RoboTechnik’s subsidiary in 2025.  
Key takeaways: 
1. Automated assembly and testing system: The company owns auto align 
technology with advanced AI / Machine Learning algorithms, which enables 
multi-axis and high-precision alignments, fulfilling customers’ requirements on 
800G+ SiPh or CPO products. For the machine vision, management mentioned that 
RoboTechnik’s accumulated experiences in image processing and alignment 
algorithms, along with the multiple camera system used, empowering the precise 
motion control of their equipment (for example, defects detection as small as 
0.5μm). 
2. SiPh and CPO opportunities: Management sees the rising end demand for SiPh 
solutions, along with increasing data volume for AI model training and higher
