China PCB: Victory Giant (300476.SZ, NC) Factory visit: AI PCB new 
capacity ramp-up and mix upgrade driven by high-speed data 
31 December 2025 | 8:33AM HKT
We visited Victory Giant’s (300476.SZ, Not Covered) HuiYang factory and met 
management on Dec 30th. Key discussions focused on PCB capacity expansion, 
technology advantages, and product mix upgrade. Overall, management is 
constructive on the company’s AI PCB growth ahead, supported by (1) new capacity 
expansion; (2) dollar content increase on mix upgrade towards new generation of 
platforms and high-speed solutions; and (3) continuous customer penetration in AI 
infrastructure. Management highlighted the company’s accumulated know-how on 
MLPCB (Multi-layer PCB) and HDI with high layers and mass production capabilities.  
Read-across to China PCB: Management comments on the company’s capacity 
expansion and mix upgrade echoes our positive view on China PCB. We expect the 
strong demand of AI PCB to benefit high-end PCB/CCL suppliers ahead, driven by 
dollar content increase of new AI server platforms with higher data transmission 
speed. We believe local PCB suppliers will continue capacity expansion to capture 
the growing demand. Buy: Shennan Circuits.  
Company profile: Victory Giant (300476.SZ) is a global leading PCB supplier offering 
MLPCB, HDI, single / double layer PCB, FPC (flexible printed circuit), etc., covering 
diversified end markets, such as AI data center, automobile, telecom, and smart 
terminals, with a global footprint in mainland China, Malaysia, Vietnam, and 
Thailand. On AI infrastructure, the company has covered solutions across AI 
accelerator card, AI server, data center switch, UBB, SSD/ DDR, and optical 
transceiver. 
Key takeaways  
1. Accelerated AI PCB capacity expansion: The company facilitates capacity 
expansion of AI PCBs within a shorter period and will continue to expand its own 
capacity for AI demand. Besides existing product lines, Victory is also working on two 
new factories with larger scale in HuiYang and is expanding capacity in overseas sites 
incl. Thailand/ Vietnam. Management noted it is taking less time for the company to 
ramp up new capacity, supported by deep partnerships with major clients and 
experiences of product verification.  
2. MLPCB and HDI accumulated know-how: The company has mass production 
capabilities of both HDI (28-layers with 8+12+8 build-up) and MLPCB (70-layers), 
benefiting from the specification upgrade of AI server new platforms. Management 
noted PCB equipment parameter and formula of materials affects the product
