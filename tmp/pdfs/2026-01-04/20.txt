Kweichow Moutai (600519.SS): Aiming for more commercialized pricing 
system, stable supply in 2026 with products quota adjustment
29 December 2025 | 11:23AM HKT
Moutai held its annual distributor meeting on Dec 28th in Guiyang. For 2026, which 
marks the beginning of China’s 15th Five-Year Plan, Moutai will proactively adapt to 
market conditions, prioritize market-oriented transformation (including pricing 
system), maintain stable overall supply to match with overall demand to avoid high 
fluctuation in pricing, and reduce higher-priced products allocation (mainly 
non-standard SKUs), while the key message is largely in line with its regional 
distributor meetings held on 11 Dec. Key messages to distributors include: 1) 
Overall supply will remain stable in 2026, with dynamic adjustment of product 
mix based on market conditions. The strategy includes dynamically coordinating 
product allocation with market demand and fully maintaining allocation structure 
with product positioning adaptation. Specifically, reducing 100ml/200ml/1000ml 
non-standard Feitian SKUs to increase standard Feitian’s market share; 
upgrading Jingpin Moutai quality to strengthen its position as the second major SKU; 
decreasing Zodiac and Vintage Moutai quota, and completely discontinuing Caiyou 
Zhenpin supply. The company will adhere to differentiated positioning of each 
product series and discontinue the distribution models from specialty stores to 
distributors for non-standard SKUs (to improve channel profitability). 2) At the price 
level, management stressed the need for reasonable and stable price expectations 
to avoid high fluctuation in wholesale prices of Feitian and other Moutai products. 
Therefore, Moutai will promote “price marketization reform” with the core principles 
of following market conditions and promoting volume-price balance. 3) Channel 
wise, Moutai will focus on enhancing channel diversity and new customer base 
expansion. The company will scientifically monitor market and channel capacity to 
effectively reduce channel inventory pressure, further activate channel vitality, and 
improve market demand adaptation. Moutai will focus on building integrated 
online-offline and traditional-modern complementary channel networks. By 
leveraging the i-Moutai platform, the company will gradually introduce reputable 
and compliant e-commerce platforms while encouraging qualified distributors to 
open online authorized stores. For the new customer base, while maintaining existing 
customer relationships, the company will closely follow new economy/new business 
consumption scenarios, as well as family banquets and private gatherings, to 
vigorously explore new demand. 4) At the service level, Moutai will focus on 
enhancing consumer experience, strengthening channel ecosystem resilience, and 
improving business capabilities. The company will scientifically deploy “three stores 
and one center” (flagship stores, image stores, specialty stores and cultural 
experience centers) and build a comprehensive anti-counterfeiting system. The 
company will also deepen its relationship with distributors, resist speculation

GS View: Since the regional distributor meeting on 11 Dec, Original case Feitian Moutai’s 
wholesale price/bottle improved to Rmb1,560 per bottle from the trough level at 
Rmb1,495, and unpacked Feitian Moutai’s wholesale price improved to Rmb1,550 from 
the trough level of Rmb1,485, while the non-standard Moutai SKUs with reduced quota 
all observed wholesale prices improvement: Zhenpin to Rmb3,250 from Rmb2,950, 
15-Year Vintage Moutai to Rmb4,020 from Rmb3,590, Zodiac Moutai to Rmb1,820 from 
Rmb1,610. Jinpin Moutai, with an enhanced strategic position within the portfolio, also 
saw its wholesale price improved to Rmb2,250 from Rmb2,000. Distributors/retailers’ 
confidence have been strengthened in the near term, also aided by the upcoming 
festival season. In the longer term to achieve a more commercialized pricing system, we 
believe that the optimization of Moutai’s direct-sales capability and more scientific 
quota re-allocation for capable distributors/SOE distributors/ParentCo’sales company 
are crucial. We maintain Buy and continue to expect 4%/3% sales & NP growth in 
2025/2026E with the stock trading at 19x 2026e with c.4% dividend yield.

Price Target Risks and Methodology - Kweichow Moutai 
Valuation methodology: Our 12m TP of Rmb1,691 is based on a 23.4x 2026E P/E, 
discounted back to 2026E year-end using an 8.5% COE. Our target P/E is benchmarked 
to the company’s average P/E in the 2012-2023 full cycle. 
Key downside risks: 1) Potential regulation changes such as a consumption tax rate 
hike; 2) Environmental pollution; 3) A slower-than-expected macroeconomic recovery; 
4) Capacity constraints; 5) More volatility in US rate hikes given the negative correlation 
between Moutai’s P/E and the US 10-yr bond yield.
